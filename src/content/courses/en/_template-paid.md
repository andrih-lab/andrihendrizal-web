Copy this file to start a new **paid** course listing. `purchaseUrl` must
point to an external platform that actually handles payment and access
(Gumroad, Payhip, Teachable, etc.) — see the note in src/content.config.ts
for why this site cannot protect paid content on its own.

Write ONLY a public teaser here: what the course covers, who it's for,
what's included (e.g. "6 video lessons, downloadable worksheet"), and
maybe a free preview clip. Do not paste the actual paid lesson content or
video into this file — it would be publicly visible to anyone, defeating
the point of it being paid.
